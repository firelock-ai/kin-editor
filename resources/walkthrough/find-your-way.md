## Find your way around

The Kin icon in the activity bar opens the Entity Explorer: the functions, types
and modules Kin found, listed from the graph rather than from a file tree.

- `Kin: Semantic Search` asks the graph a question in your own words.
- `Kin: Trace Entity` shows what a symbol touches and what touches it.
- `Kin: Review Current File` reviews the open file against the graph.
- `Kin: Graph Overview` reports what is indexed right now.

Hover and go-to-definition answer from the graph too, and only while the MCP
connection is live. The status bar carries daemon and graph health.

Keyboard, with `Ctrl` on Windows and Linux and `Cmd` on macOS:
`Ctrl+Shift+K S` searches, `Ctrl+Shift+K T` traces, `Ctrl+Shift+K O` shows the
overview, `Ctrl+Shift+K V` reviews, `Ctrl+Shift+K W` opens setup.
